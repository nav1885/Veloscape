## Full description (App Store + Google Play — both ≤4000 chars)

**Veloscape is the AI coach that rides in your ear.**

Strava shows you a number on a screen — after it's too late to react. Garmin and Wahoo alerts beep at you with raw metrics. Veloscape actually coaches you: as you approach, ride, and finish each of your starred Strava segments, it speaks to you in real time, knows your history on that exact segment, and tells you what to do differently this time.

No screen-staring. No fiddling. Connect Strava, pick how you want to ride, and go.

**ONE TAP TO START**
Choose your mode and start riding — Veloscape coaches every starred segment on your route automatically:
• PR — aggressive, go-for-it pacing when you're chasing a personal record
• Training — balanced guidance for a solid everyday effort
• Recovery — intentionally quiet; it stays out of your way so you keep it easy

**REAL-TIME VOICE COACHING**
• Approach cues fire before each segment — your PR target and what to watch for
• In-segment calls tell you if you're up or down on pace, so you can respond while it still matters
• Result cues call your time the moment you cross — including new PRs
• Hands-free and earbud-friendly (works great with bone-conduction headphones)

**KNOWS YOUR HISTORY**
Veloscape pulls your starred segments and past efforts from Strava, so the coaching is personal — built around how *you* ride each segment, not generic numbers.

**EVERY RIDE, BROKEN DOWN**
• A clear post-ride summary of how you did, segment by segment
• Full ride history with route maps — tap any segment to see your stretch on the map and how this effort compares to your past
• Your performance trend on each segment over time

**WHO IT'S FOR**
Segment hunters, PR chasers, and anyone who races the same climbs and loops and wants a coach in their ear instead of a number on a screen.

Requires a free Strava account. Location is used during rides to track your route and detect segments; it's never used for ads and never sold. See our privacy policy: https://nav1885.github.io/Veloscape/privacy.html

Ride smarter. Let Veloscape call the shots.
