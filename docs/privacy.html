Veloscape Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 2, 2026  ·  Last updated: June 2, 2026

Veloscape (“Veloscape,” “we,” “us”) is a cycling coaching app that tracks your rides, detects your starred Strava segments, and delivers real-time audio coaching. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. We do not sell your personal data and we do not show ads.

1. Information We Collect

Strava account data (with your permission)

When you connect your Strava account, you authorize Veloscape (via Strava’s OAuth) to access: your Strava profile (name, athlete ID, profile photo), your starred segments, and your activities and segment efforts. We use this to personalize coaching and compare your performance over time. You can revoke this access at any time in your Strava settings or by signing out in the app.

Location data

During a ride, Veloscape collects precise GPS location from your device to record your route, detect when you enter and exit segments, and trigger audio coaching cues. Location is collected only while a ride is active.

Ride and performance data

We process the routes, times, speeds, and segment efforts generated during your rides to provide coaching feedback and post-ride summaries.

App settings

Your preferences (e.g., text-to-speech settings, selected coaching mode) are stored locally on your device.

What we do NOT collect

  • No advertising identifiers and no third-party advertising.
  • No third-party analytics, tracking, or behavioral profiling SDKs.
  • We never sell or rent your personal information.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Track your rides and detect your starred segments in real time.
  • Generate audio coaching cues and post-ride summaries.
  • Compare your current effort against your past performance on the same segments.
  • Maintain your authenticated session with Strava.

3. Background Location

Veloscape can access location in the background — that is, while the app is not on screen or your phone is locked — only during an active ride. This is required so that ride tracking, segment detection, and audio coaching continue uninterrupted when your screen is off or you switch apps mid-ride. Background location is never used when a ride is not in progress, and is never used for advertising or shared with data brokers.

4. How Your Information Is Stored

Your rides, segments, and settings are stored locally on your device. Authentication tokens are kept in your device’s secure storage. To provide coaching and summaries, certain ride and segment data is sent to our backend server for processing (see Third Parties below).

5. Third Parties & Service Providers

We share data only with the services needed to operate the app:

  • Strava — the source of your account, segment, and activity data, accessed with your authorization under Strava’s API terms and privacy policy.
  • Anthropic (Claude) — our backend sends ride and segment context to Anthropic’s API to generate personalized coaching cues and ride summaries. See Anthropic’s privacy policy.
  • Hosting infrastructure — our backend runs on a cloud hosting provider that processes data on our behalf to deliver the service.

We do not share your personal information with any other parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

6. Data Retention & Deleting Your Data

Ride data stored on your device remains until you delete it or uninstall the app. You can:

  • Sign out in the app to clear your session and disconnect your Strava authorization;
  • Uninstall the app to remove locally stored data from your device;
  • Revoke access to Veloscape anytime from your Strava account settings;
  • Request deletion of any data held on our backend by emailing us (see Contact). We will delete it within 30 days.

7. Security

We use industry-standard measures, including encrypted transport (HTTPS) and secure on-device token storage, to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to safeguard your data.

8. Children’s Privacy

Veloscape is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your jurisdiction) and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Your Rights

Depending on where you live (e.g., the EEA/UK under GDPR, or California under the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or port your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

10. International Users

Your information may be processed in the United States or other countries where our service providers operate. By using Veloscape you consent to this processing.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will revise the “Last updated” date above and, for material changes, provide notice in the app or via the connected email.
